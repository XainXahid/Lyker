Fee simple vs leasehold?

Fee simple means you own the home and the land under it, with no end date.

Leasehold means you own the home but lease the land from a landowner until a set year, and you pay monthly lease rent. It's common in older Waikīkī and Kāhala properties. Leasehold homes cost less up front, but they get harder to finance and resell as the end date gets closer. When the lease ends, the property can go back to the landowner unless the land (the "fee") is bought or the lease is extended.
